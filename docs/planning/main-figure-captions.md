# Figure Captions

## Figure 1. Candidate Transit Route Map

Candidate spring transit route segments used to contextualize the main
diagnostic analysis, shown over a Natural Earth coastline layer in
longitude-latitude coordinates. Circles mark route starts, crosses mark route
ends, and the star marks the approximate Zeebrugge breeding colony location.

## Figure 2. Batch Time-Warp Robustness

Mean distance sensitivity under artificial temporal reparameterization across
20 transit-focused candidate spring migration trajectories. Each trajectory
was compared with monotone time-warped versions generated using the exponent
\(\gamma\), where \(\gamma=1\) corresponds to the unwarped trajectory. Lines
show mean distance to the original trajectory, and shaded bands indicate one
standard deviation.

## Figure 3. Season-Aware Normalized Perturbation Sweep

Median metric-specific responses to controlled shape perturbations relative to
each trajectory's mean response to strong time-warp controls
(\(\gamma=0.4\) and \(\gamma=2.5\)). The sweep includes all transit-focused
routes with at least 80 points: 175 spring trajectories and 172 autumn
trajectories. Each row shows one season, and columns show smoothing,
local-detour, and local-loop intensity sweeps. The dashed horizontal line marks
the time-warp baseline.
